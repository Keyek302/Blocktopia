🎲 How to play Craps

Everyone bets on the roll of two dice. The simplest bet is the Pass Line.

1. Come-out roll. 7 or 11 wins. 2, 3 or 12 loses (“craps”). Any other number (4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10) becomes the point, and the ON puck moves to it.

2. Roll again. Hit the point number before a 7 and you win. Roll a 7 first and you lose (“seven out”). Then a new round starts.

Don’t Pass is the opposite. It wins on 2 or 3 and loses on 7 or 11 at the come-out. 12 is a tie. Once there’s a point, it wins if a 7 comes first.

Field (one roll): wins on 2, 3, 4, 9, 10, 11 or 12. Loses on 5, 6, 7, 8.

Any Seven (one roll): wins only if the dice total 7, pays 4:1.

Odds (after a point is set): add up to 3× your Pass Line bet behind it. It wins along with your Pass bet at true odds, so the house has no edge on it: 2:1 on 4 or 10, 3:2 on 5 or 9, 6:5 on 6 or 8.

Place bets: tap a point number to bet it rolls before a 7. Pays 9:5 on 4 or 10, 7:5 on 5 or 9, 7:6 on 6 or 8. They only work while a point is set, and they stay up after a win until a 7 rolls.

Tip: Pass Line is the easiest to learn and has some of the best odds in the casino.
